Product advantages

  • Two independent DC electromagnetic disc brakes provide D8 Plus redundancy.
  • IP66 cast-aluminium housing protects the mechanism from dust and high-pressure water.
  • An adjustable overload clutch sits outside the load-bearing path.
  • Precision helical gearing supports smooth operation and long service life.
  • The external polyamide chain guide is self-lubricating, wear resistant and easy to inspect.
  • V100 load chain supports dependable performance in demanding stage environments.
