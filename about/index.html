About RIGMA

Confidence
above the stage.

RIGMA is a professional stage lifting brand focused on reliable equipment, coordinated systems and clear long-term support for the global entertainment industry.

Our purpose

We help entertainment professionals lift with greater confidence—from the individual hoist to the complete operating system.

Every production depends on equipment that performs consistently under real working conditions. RIGMA brings together stage hoists, control, transport and system accessories around the needs of professional users: safety, repeatability, efficient deployment and dependable technical communication.

02
