Product advantages

  • Compact purpose-built stage design reduces occupied space, handling effort and transport load.
  • IP66 high-pressure die-cast housing supports demanding production environments.
  • Double-layer chain guard improves chain entry and exit stability and helps prevent jamming.
  • Precision helical gearing and an external polyamide chain guide support smooth operation.
  • Single- or dual-brake configurations are available to suit the required application.
